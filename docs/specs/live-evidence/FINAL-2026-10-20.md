## Orphans and shutdown

- Graceful `SIGTERM` to the release daemon with a live Chromium page:
  all Chrome/Helper processes for the profile were gone after the drain.
- The forced-exit path (second SIGTERM/SIGINT during the drain) now
  reaps every `ProcessOwner::Browser` child through the supervisor
  **before** `process::exit` (`crates/terminal/src/browser_reap.rs`,
  `crates/cli/src/daemon/serve.rs::on_force_signal`). Regression tests:
  `sigterm_to_the_daemon_leaves_no_browser_process` (real SIGTERM to
  `serve_impl` with a browser-owned child) and
  `force_exit_kills_browser_children_before_process_exit` (force hook with
  a browser-owned child).
- SIGKILL: a live `kill -KILL` of the macOS daemon left 18 Chromium
  processes (reproduced and then cleaned). macOS provides no
  parent-death signal; the Linux guardian + `PR_SET_PDEATHSIG` and the
  Windows kill-on-close job are the platform authorities, but macOS has
  no equivalent. This is a typed platform boundary: forced
  SIGTERM/SIGINT is fully reaped, SIGKILL cannot run a destructor and is
  documented rather than claimed. (An operator `kill -9` leaves the
  browser tree; the profile directory and artifacts remain removable.)
