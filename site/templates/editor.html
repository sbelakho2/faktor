Thirty-six specified screens

The complete flow, beyond the hero.

  1. 01

    Capture and preserve the task.

    Project readiness, binary discovery, native start consent, typed task criteria, context attachments and captured finish choices.

  2. 02

    Work with bounded authority.

    Named current work, stop conditions and target/reason/scope permission review. No blanket grant is implied.

  3. 03

    Inspect the evaluated candidate.

    Plan, agents, all evidence references, review/verification, changed files and matching verified/landed identities.

  4. 04

    Recover coherently.

    Ambiguous submission, reconnect, malformed durable frame, unavailable proof, missing cost and SCM blockers retain useful state.

  5. 05

    Record the outcome.

    History separates conversation completion, engineering acceptance, integration, requested steps, final basis and recorded cost.

Keyboard and recovery

Small details that keep the work usable.

Interaction contracts for keyboard, layout, refresh and recovery.
ConditionInteraction contract
KeyboardRoving main tabs, visible focus, Ctrl/Cmd + Enter scoped to the composer, Escape for confirmation recovery.
Narrow paneWrap paths and key/value rows, stack composer actions, move only secondary controls to accessible overflow.
Async refreshRetain item identity and coherent snapshot; do not steal focus or skip an unprocessed durable frame.
SubmissionOne admitted logical request; keep the captured draft, finish and context when the response is ambiguous.
Unavailable informationShow unavailable proof/cost explicitly. Unavailable cost is not zero.
DiagnosticsMask credentials; copied support details are redacted.
