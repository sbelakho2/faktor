Legal / Privacy

Privacy, in bounded terms.

What the website stores, why it stores it, and what it refuses to collect.

Last updated / 12 October 2026

Data we store

  • Account identity: email address, display name, password hash and email-verification state.
  • Organization membership and role.
  • Session records: a hash of the session token, CSRF token, timestamps and a coarse client identifier.
  • Billing records: plan, subscription state, exact minor-unit amounts, invoices, receipts, payment-provider references and masked payment-method details.

Data we do not store

We do not store raw card numbers, card verification codes or full provider payment payloads. Payment-method records contain only the provider token, brand, last four digits and expiry that the provider supplies.

Cookies

The account surface uses an HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax session cookie and an anonymous CSRF cookie before sign-in. The marketing site uses no analytics or tracking scripts. No JavaScript runs.

Retention

Session records are revoked on sign-out and expire after the configured session lifetime. Email tokens expire and are single-use. Billing records are retained for the accounting period required by the operating entity.

Processors

Mollie processes payments and notifies Faktor with a payment id; Faktor fetches the referenced payment from Mollie before applying any state. Email delivery is performed by the configured mail transport. Both receive only the fields needed for the operation.

Your choices

You can request account export or deletion through the operating entity. A deletion request cannot erase records that must be retained for tax or fraud-prevention obligations; the response names the retained basis.
