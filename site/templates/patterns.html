Failure repair

Start with the counterexample.

  1. 01

    Attach the failing basis.

    Keep the relevant test or observed failure alongside the task.

  2. 02

    Constrain the repair.

    State preserved behavior, affected scope and the requested verification.

  3. 03

    Review before landing.

    Inspect changed files, unrelated changes and the evidence for the candidate being integrated.
