Useful boundaries

Before you begin.

Does Faktor require another Bel Consulting product?

No. Native intelligence and verification form the baseline. Optional providers can enrich neutral interfaces.

Does a passing check mean the whole change is verified?

A result has a scope. Inspect the required criteria, named claims, candidate identity, integration state and final basis together. An empty set of required checks can pass without establishing a verified change.

What stays in the IDE?

The product uses the host’s theme and typography. Work, Inspect and History are shared vocabulary across its native surfaces.
