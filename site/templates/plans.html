Billing rules

Provider truth upstream. Faktor billing truth.

Adyen reports payment outcomes by signed webhook. Faktor records entitlements, invoices and receipts locally. A pending checkout never becomes a subscription because a browser returned successfully.

Checkout

Hosted handoff

Faktor creates a provider payment link server-side and redirects the browser. Payment details stay on the provider page.

Webhook

Signed and idempotent

HMAC signature, timestamp window and event identity are checked before durable state changes.

Record

Exact and durable

Invoices, receipts and payments use integer minor units. The account surface shows the recorded basis.
