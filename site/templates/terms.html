Terms of use.

The operational terms for a Faktor account, the hosted website and the billing record.

Last updated / 12 October 2026

1. The service

Faktor is an engineering agent interface and runtime. The website documents the product, provides account access and hosts plan checkout. A browser session does not start a model request, change a repository or grant repository permissions.

2. Accounts

You must provide an accurate email address, keep your password confidential and verify your email before billing actions. You are responsible for activity under your account and for the members you invite to an organization.

3. Plans and payment

Plans are billed in euro in advance. Prices exclude VAT unless the account page says otherwise. Checkout is handled by Adyen; Faktor does not receive or store raw card data. Subscriptions renew until cancelled. Plan changes and cancellations take effect on the date recorded in the account area.

4. Invoices and refunds

Invoices and receipts are available in the account area. Refund requests are reviewed against the recorded payment and invoice state. A provider refund does not rewrite the original invoice.

5. Acceptable use

Do not attempt to bypass authentication, cross organization boundaries, replay webhooks or interfere with the evidence record. Security testing requires written authorization.

6. Availability

The website and account surfaces are provided as-is. A pending payment webhook can delay entitlement; the account page reports the state rather than assuming success.

7. Contact

Account and billing questions: support through the Bel Consulting contact channel supplied with your order. This edition is a working implementation; no external contact URL is invented here.
