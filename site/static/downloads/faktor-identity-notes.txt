Faktor visual identity — usage rules and source analysis
Edition 4 / 12 October 2026

Source: Faktor_Website.pdf, pages 29–33 (current identity) and 46–50 (retained identity).

LOGO
- Fixed outlined artwork. Do not retype, stretch, rotate or add effects.
- Preferred symbol size: 24 px. Use the supplied icon-only mark below that context.
- Clear space: at least one quarter of the symbol height (H/4) on every side.
- Ink on Paper and Ochre on Ink are the two production masters.

PALETTE
Ink #08110C — primary surface, type and dark bands.
Forest #143D2B — structural dark surface.
Paper #F3F5EF — reading surface.
Sage #92AA99 — metadata on Ink.
Ochre #D9BA75 — accent on Ink and warm highlight.
Ochre Deep #72541E — readable accent on Paper.
State colours (success, warning, danger) keep semantic meaning; they are not brand accents.

TYPOGRAPHY
Barlow Semi Condensed 600 for display and page headings.
Barlow 400–600 for the reading surface.
IBM Plex Mono 400–500 for technical metadata, labels and commands.
The logo wordmark is fixed artwork; host editors retain their own typography.

APPLICATION
- The marketing site is server-rendered with no JavaScript.
- The editor prototype uses CSS-only radio state for host, theme and evidence state.
- Work / Inspect / History are the shared vocabulary across native host surfaces.
- The identity does not overwrite the host's theme, focus tokens or editor typography.
